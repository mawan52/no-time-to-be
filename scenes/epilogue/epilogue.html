NO TIME TO BE


MAWAN52

A Digital Artwork on Youth Burnout

Teenagers today face constant pressure to excel, overwhelmed by a endless barrage of schoolwork, messaging apps, social media, health trackers, and anxiety about their futures.

When every expectation demands 100% of your energy, one eventually loses control and the tasks begin controlling you.

Pleasing everyone is a losing trade.
Focus on being present for the ones who you truly cherish.
